# M5.1 and M6 story/gameplay plan — review draft

**Status: Superseded (Nov 14).** The team answered these questions; the decisions now live in `docs/SPEC.md` (§3.3, §3.5, §5), `docs/ROADMAP.md` (M5.1–M9) and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Notable change from this draft: M6 is one *large* Underworld region with forts and sentries, and Hades gets a full 6-move kit.

## Proposed direction

Open the Underworld story at the Outer Grotto. In the M5.1 story beat, Hades meets Zeus after Olympus falls and teleports them together to the grotto. There, they discover that humans captured the real Cerberus and are draining him to power the Cerberus Mk. II. The first objective becomes **“Find and free Cerberus.”**

Hades has a brief story appearance in M5.1, but his **gameplay introduction is M6**: he joins as a companion, follows and helps under AI control, and the player can switch between Zeus and Hades. This is the working interpretation of “introduce Hades in M6.” If Hades should not appear in the story until M6, the M5.1 portal beat needs a different treatment.

## M5.1 — story transition and grotto discovery

1. **Finish M5 first.** Keep the existing tutorial and Olympus rocket sequence intact and packaged before this follow-up starts.
2. **Hades reaches Zeus after the destruction.** He explains that the surface is unsafe and proposes the Underworld as a place to regroup.
3. **Hades teleports them together.** Use a short, readable portal transition with input locked only for the scene. Resume control smoothly at the destination.
4. **Begin in the Outer Grotto as Zeus.** Hades is present as a story character, but has no follower AI or character switching in this milestone.
5. **Reveal the rescue hook.** A trace, recording, or visible energy link tells them the humans have captured the real Cerberus to power the Cerberus Mk. II. Set the objective to **“Find and free Cerberus.”**
6. **End M5.1 in playable control at the grotto.** The grotto is the first Underworld safe area and the starting point for M6 exploration.

**M5.1 does not include:** Hades combat, switchable characters, companion AI, soldier camps, or a Cerberus boss/rescue encounter.

## M6 — Hades joins; first Underworld exploration

### Step-by-step build order

1. **Lock the M5.1 checkpoint.** Run the existing milestone gates and package the story arrival before beginning M6.
2. **Add Hades as a second sim character.** Keep his health, stamina, weapon, abilities, and position in serializable sim state. Spawn him through the normal registry and keep gameplay outcomes authority-gated.
3. **Add character switching.** Introduce a rebindable `switchCharacter` action. V is only a proposed default; switching changes the locally controlled character through an intent. The other god remains in the world.
4. **Give Hades a signature weapon.** Proposed first weapon: an Underworld bident built from the existing spear/weapon system, with a Shadow element and distinct generated appearance. Support its light and heavy attacks through the existing combat pipeline. Keep its first version to one weapon; additional weapons and Hades fusion discoveries can follow later.
5. **Build Hades’s two signature skills from the spec.** `Shadow Step` gives him a short evasive reposition. `Skull Cage` controls an area after a clear, fair warning. Both use the existing rebindable input map, status/damage pipeline, cooldowns, accessibility rules, and deterministic sim.
6. **Make the inactive god a useful companion.** Start with simple authored-route following, catch-up when obstructed, and basic nearby-combat help. Do not build a command wheel, broad tactical AI, or navigation for every biome yet.
7. **Make one compact Underworld route playable.** Extend from the Outer Grotto toward a visible human-held Cerberus site. The route can demonstrate seeded terrain, exploration reveal, and basic navigation, but should not require the complete six-biome world or the interior mech encounter.
8. **Save and restore the team.** A reload preserves both gods, their positions and equipment, the selected character, the grotto discovery, and the rescue objective.
9. **Package M6 before moving on.** Complete the milestone's unit, browser, visual-review, build, and package gates. The demo must still load and start reliably on itch.io.

### Hades’s M6 minimum kit

| Part | First playable version | Later expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Shadow bident; light/heavy attacks using existing spear support | More weapon types, recipes, and Hades-specific fusions |
| Mobility skill | Shadow Step | Variants, upgrades, and combo branches |
| Control skill | Skull Cage with a visible warning before it takes effect | More crowd-control skills and richer enemy reactions |
| Companion behavior | Follow, catch up, and assist against nearby threats | Tactics, commands, and wider-world navigation |

The skill names and fair-warning requirement match the current game spec. The bident is a proposed presentation and content choice, not yet a locked decision.

## M7 and later — keep the rescue encounter in its own milestone

- **M7: Human threat and Cerberus rescue.** Add the required soldiers and approach, then build the Cerberus Mk. II encounter, rear-plating/hatch sequence, pilot fight, and Cerberus release. Test the encounter with either god active and the other assisting. Do not pull the mech interior or rescue boss into M6.
- **M8: Demo hardening.** Polish the first complete loop, accessibility, captions/audio, saves, performance, itch.io packaging, and the published-build smoke check.
- **After the demo slice:** Expand toward the full six-biome world, broader resource/crafting progression, camps, base building, additional Hades equipment/skills, and other mechs. These systems remain part of the larger game vision; they should not block the first complete grotto-to-Cerberus loop.

## Roadmap review

The current roadmap puts broad six-biome generation, resource gathering, crafting, and the full map in M6, while Hades is deferred to M9. That ordering no longer matches this direction. The proposed change is to bring **Hades’s playable introduction and starter kit into M6**, keep **the Cerberus rescue in M7**, and move broad world expansion behind the first complete demo loop. M6 should establish enough reusable world-generation and map behavior for the Underworld slice without trying to finish every biome at the same time.

This is a deliberate scope trade: Hades’s switching, companion behavior, weapon, and two skills are substantial work. Retaining all of the current broad M6 list as well would make the milestone too large and put the demo build at risk.

## Decisions to review before implementation

1. **Hades’s first appearance:** story cameo in M5.1, playable companion in M6 (recommended), or no appearance before M6?
2. **Hades’s first kit:** one Shadow bident plus Shadow Step and Skull Cage (recommended), with more arsenal content later?
3. **Companion baseline:** follow and basic nearby-combat help (recommended), or follow only?
4. **Switch control:** use V as the proposed rebindable default, or select another key?
5. **M6 environment size:** one polished Underworld route to the Cerberus site (recommended), or retain the full six-biome M6 scope?

Once these choices are reviewed, update `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` together before implementation begins. Keep the M5.1 and M6 code work separate and complete each milestone's run/package gate before starting the next.
