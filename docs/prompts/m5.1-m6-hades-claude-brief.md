## Agreed game direction

- The post-rocket story moves to the **Outer Grotto in the Underworld**.
- Hades meets Zeus and teleports them both there. The real Cerberus has been captured by humans and is being used to power the **Cerberus Mk. II** mech.
- The immediate objective is **“Find and free Cerberus.”** The grotto is the starting safe area.
- Hades’s story appearance and his gameplay introduction are separate beats: M5.1 handles the arrival and discovery; M6 makes him a switchable playable god and helpful AI companion.
- Hades needs a distinct weapon and skills. The proposed M6 starter kit is one Shadow bident with light/heavy attacks, `Shadow Step`, and `Skull Cage` with a clear warning before it takes effect.
- Keep the Cerberus mech encounter and rescue for M7. Do not jump ahead and implement the boss or mech interior in M6.

## Implement one milestone at a time

### M5.1 — story transition and grotto discovery

1. Preserve the finished M5 tutorial and rocket sequence.
2. After Olympus falls, have Hades meet Zeus and explain that the surface is unsafe. Hades opens a portal and brings both gods to the Outer Grotto.
3. Show a short, calm transition; lock input only during the scene and return control smoothly at the grotto. Respect reduced-motion settings and caption dialogue and important sound cues.
4. Start the player in control of Zeus. Hades may appear as a scripted story character/NPC, but M5.1 does not add companion AI, switching, or Hades combat.
5. Reveal that the humans captured the real Cerberus and are draining him to power the Cerberus Mk. II. Set the objective to **“Find and free Cerberus.”** Use the simplest clear in-world clue and record that choice.
6. Save the story state so a reload preserves the grotto start, clue, and objective.
7. Finish and package M5.1 before starting M6. Do not include soldier camps, a mech fight, the mech interior, or the Cerberus rescue in this milestone.

### M6 — playable Hades and the first Underworld route

After M5.1 passes its milestone gates and packages successfully:

1. Add Hades as a second serializable character using the existing spawn and sim architecture. Keep gameplay state out of meshes and `userData`.
2. Add rebindable character switching through an `Intent`. The selected character receives player input; the other remains present as an AI companion.
3. Implement Hades’s starter weapon using the agreed design. Reuse the weapon/content registry and existing combat pipeline for light and heavy attacks; do not add a one-off engine path just for Hades.
4. Implement `Shadow Step` and `Skull Cage` as deterministic sim abilities. Use the existing cooldown, damage/status, intent, and input-map patterns. Skull Cage must telegraph before it takes effect. Respect reduced motion; do not flash or strobe.
5. Give the inactive god simple, useful behavior: follow the active character, catch up if blocked, and help against nearby threats. Use deterministic sim logic and authority-gate gameplay outcomes. Do not add runtime AI services, command wheels, or broad tactical AI.
6. Build the agreed Underworld route from the Outer Grotto toward a visible Cerberus Mk. II site. Include only the map/reveal features needed to navigate that slice. Keep the actual mech encounter for M7.
7. Save and restore both gods, their locations and equipment, the selected character, and the discovery/objective state.
8. Keep visual content generated in code. Follow the approved-content and preview rules; do not add downloaded assets or binaries.
