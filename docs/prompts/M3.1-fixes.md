## 1. Bug: the player falls into the hillside and gets stuck
**Root cause (found in review):** `src/sim/systems/hero.ts` enforces the meadow edge *after* the physics move by scaling `position.x/z` back inside `MEADOW_RADIUS`, without fixing `y`. Near the edge the ground mostly rises toward the center, so the pull-back puts the feet **below the terrain**. The next tick `PhysicsWorld.syncFrom` teleports the capsule there, inside the heightfield, where the character controller can't push it back out.

Fix:
- **Never teleport for the boundary.** Before calling `physics.moveCharacter`, remove the outward part of the movement delta when the player is at or past the edge (a soft wall: they slide along it). Optionally add a gentle push-back zone over the last 5 m.
- **Safety net:** after each move, if the feet are more than 0.3 m below `groundHeightAt(x, z)`, snap them to the ground and zero vertical velocity. Also keep a "last safe grounded position" (updated every 0.5 s while grounded, not in water and not at the edge). If the hero is stuck (no movement for 1 s while trying to move, or below the ground or water level − 5), restore the last safe position.
- **Feedback at the edge:** while the prologue is active, a caption: "The rest of the world can wait. For now." After the rocket the meadow edge goes away anyway (M6).
- **Tests:** a Vitest test that drives the hero outward along the whole meadow edge (every 10° around the circle) and asserts the feet never go below the ground. A Playwright flow test that holds forward toward the edge for 5 s and checks the player is still above ground and can walk back.

## 2. Bug: sheep walk through rocks
**Root cause:** `moveWildlife` in `src/sim/systems/wildlife.ts` writes positions straight from `groundHeightAt` and ignores physics, even though creatures already get capsules in `PhysicsWorld.syncFrom`.

Fix:
- Move wildlife through `physics.moveCharacter` like the hero (with gravity / snap-to-ground), so rocks, the hero rock and other creatures block them.
- Wander targets and arrival spots must avoid rock footprints (reject spots inside any rock's radius + 0.5 m). If a sheep is blocked for 1 s, pick a new target.
- **Tests:** a Vitest test simulating 2 minutes of wandering with fixed seeds, asserting no sheep's center is ever inside a rock collider; plus a fleeing test (hero chases a sheep into a rock and the sheep goes around it).

## 3. Lightning abilities (update SPEC §5 to match)
**Root cause of "only fires once":** Chain Lightning has a 4 s cooldown, and it **fizzles without spending anything when there's no target** in 14 m, so pressing it often seems to do nothing.

New Zeus kit (Lightning element, all through intents and the damage pipeline):

| Ability | Default key | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Chain Lightning** | **Q** (tap or **hold**) | Tap: one bolt. **Hold: keeps firing every 0.3 s until Q is released or stamina hits 0.** 8 stamina per bolt, no cooldown beyond the fire rate. With a target in range it chains as now (up to 4 targets, 80% falloff). **With no target it still fires**: the bolt goes forward along the camera aim to 14 m and strikes the ground or the first thing hit (small spark burst, 50% damage in a 1.5 m radius). Running dry triggers "exhausted": stamina regen waits 1.2 s instead of 0.5 s. |
| **Sky Strike** (supercharged) | **R** | Needs ≥ 50% stamina, and **drains all of it**. A glowing ring marks the target point (the lock-on target, otherwise 12 m ahead on the ground) for 0.6 s while clouds darken, then a huge bolt hits from the sky: 5 m radius, damage = 1.2 × stamina spent, knockback, 1.5 s stun, and scorches the grass briefly. 12 s cooldown. One slow bright swell, **never strobing**; with reduce motion on, no camera shake and a softened flash. |
| **Punch** | **A** (also left mouse) | Fast 3-hit fist combo (jab, cross, uppercut). Low damage, 3 stamina per hit, works whatever weapon is held. |
| **Lightning Fist** | **D** (hold to charge, release) | Charge up to 1 s (crackling fist, rising hum). Release: a short forward lunge (2–4 m by charge) and an electrified punch: 20–45 damage by charge, knockback, and a small arc to 1 nearby enemy. 15–30 stamina by charge, 2 s cooldown. |
| **Weapon strike** | **S** (tap light, hold heavy; also right mouse = heavy) | The existing spear light/heavy attacks. |

Add each as an `AbilityModule` (or attack spec) under `content/gods/zeus/`. Add VFX in `view/effects/lightning.ts` and synthesized SFX. Show the four ability icons with their keys, cooldowns and "needs stamina" states in the HUD.

## 4. Dodge roll: faster and snappier
- Roll is **X** (keep Alt as a second binding).
- 0.26 s at 17 m/s (about 4.4 m), i-frames for the first 0.2 s, 18 stamina. A visible tumble animation on Zeus with a dust puff.
- **Input buffer** 0.15 s (pressing roll just before an attack or landing ends still rolls). Rolling can **cancel the recovery** of punches, weapon strikes and Lightning Fist.
- Roll goes in the movement direction, or backward when no direction is held. It works on slopes and doesn't push the player into terrain (same safety net as §1).

## 5. Controls: arrow keys move, letters are actions
New default bindings in `src/engine/input-map.ts` (all still rebindable in settings; also update SPEC §5 and the README controls):

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | **Arrow keys** (WASD no longer move) |
| Jump / hover | Space |
| Sprint | Shift |
| Dodge roll | X (also Alt) |
| Punch | A, left mouse |
| Lightning Fist | D (hold) |
| Weapon strike | S (tap / hold), right mouse = heavy |
| Chain Lightning | Q (tap / hold) |
| Sky Strike | R |
| Spirit ability (later) | E |
| Interact (later) | F |
| Lock-on | Tab |
| Turn camera | Mouse, **or Comma / Period** |
| Settings | Esc |
| Reserved for later | I inventory, C codex, M map |

Because the right hand is on the arrows, **the game must play well without the mouse**:
- The camera **auto-follows behind Zeus** while moving (smoothly, after 0.6 s without camera input), and Comma/Period orbit it. The mouse still works when used.
- Left/Right arrows move sideways relative to the camera; with auto-follow this feels like steering.
- Add a settings toggle "Camera auto-follow" (on by default).
- Prevent the browser's default for the arrow keys and Space (no page scrolling inside the itch.io iframe).
- Update tutorial prompts and HUD key hints to read from the input map.

**Tests:** a Playwright flow test that ArrowUp moves the player and W doesn't; holding Q fires several bolts and stops when stamina reaches 0; R drains stamina to 0 and damages a dummy inside the ring; X rolls at least 4 m; A damages a dummy; holding D longer does more damage. Vitest tests for the Chain Lightning fire rate, Sky Strike damage scaling and the roll buffer.
